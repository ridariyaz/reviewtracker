# ReviewTracker Features & Business Guide

## Overview
ReviewTracker is a powerful reputation management and review acceleration module built for physical businesses (retail stores, clinics, restaurants, service providers, repair shops).

---

## Key Business Value

### 📈 1. Accelerated Google Review Growth
- **Frictionless Review Creation**: Customers rarely write reviews because they don't know what to type. ReviewTracker autogenerates high-quality, realistic reviews on the spot.
- **Local SEO Rank Optimization**: Every review includes keywords that Google Maps and Google Search use for local ranking (`"best local shop"`, `"fast checkout"`, `"fair prices"`, `"clean store"`).
- **1-Tap Clipboard Copy**: One tap copies the review and opens the business's Google Review page directly.

### 🛡️ 2. Public Reputation Shielding
- **Negative Feedback Defense**: Unhappy customers are routed to a private internal feedback form instead of Google.
- **1-Tap Issue Chips**: Customers can tap issues in 1 second (⏱️ *Wait Time*, 💬 *Communication*, 💵 *Pricing*, 📦 *Stock*, 🧹 *Cleanliness*) instead of abandoning the form.
- **Private Log & Alert**: Management receives the private feedback directly in the admin dashboard.
